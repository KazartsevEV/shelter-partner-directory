Separate evidence from assumptions

A supplier quote can support a purchase cost. A planned number of orders is still an assumption until you have evidence from customers. Keep that distinction when entering your scenario.

Start with a product or service in V1. Review the resulting costs, price and financial forecast. For several offerings with shared expenses, use the separate V2 portfolio.

Try a lower sales assumption or a higher cost. Compare the projected profit, cash needs and remaining inventory before deciding which inputs need more evidence.

Test the assumptions with customers

The calculator evaluates the numbers you enter. It does not research demand or predict whether people will buy. Customer conversations and actual selling tests remain separate work.
