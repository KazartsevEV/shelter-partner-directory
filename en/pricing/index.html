What belongs in your pricing scenario?

QuestionWhat to prepare
What are you selling?Choose a product or service. For a product, distinguish manufacturing, resale and dropshipping.
What does delivery of one sale cost?List purchasing or materials, work, logistics and related expenses supported by your chosen branch.
Which costs support the business?Identify staff, equipment and operating expenses. For V2, distinguish shared and individual costs.
How will customers find you?Enter advertising and conversion assumptions. Do not treat a forecast as confirmed demand.
What will you retain?Review the applicable target margin, taxes and financing settings. Margin and markup are different measures.

Use the branch that matches your work

V1 models one product or service. V2 models several offerings with shared and individual expenses. Use the existing choice in the calculator to select the route that matches your business.
